# Toss Away

**English** · [Español](es/index) · [Português](pt-br/index) · [Français](fr/index)

Toss Away reads your screenshots on your iPhone and sorts them by what they
say: one-time codes, receipts, chats, maps, shopping, social posts and app
screens. Card numbers, IBANs and ID numbers land in a **Sensitive info** group,
recognised by their checksums rather than by guesswork.

- **Search inside your screenshots** — find a code, an address or a total by
  the text in the image.
- **Receipts to CSV** — totals, merchants and dates, ready for a spreadsheet.
- **Near duplicates** — repeated shots of the same screen, grouped together.
- **Act on what was read** — copy a code, open an address in Maps, call a
  number.

When a group has served its purpose, delete what you no longer need — from
the list, or one at a time with the optional Quick review. Nothing is deleted
until you confirm in Trash.

Everything is read on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no
account.

## Free and Pro

Every category, search and one-by-one review are free, with no daily limit.
**Pro** adds Delete all for a whole group, receipt export to CSV and
near-duplicate groups.
