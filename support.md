## Common questions

### What does Toss Away do?

It reads the text in your screenshots, on your iPhone, and sorts them by what
they say — codes, receipts, chats, maps, shopping, social posts, app screens,
and a Sensitive info group for card numbers, IBANs and ID numbers. You can
search inside them, export receipts to CSV and see near-duplicates together.

### Can I try it without screenshots of my own?

Yes. On the empty screen, tap **Try with sample screenshots**. Toss Away writes
38 example screenshots into a "Toss Away Samples" album and sorts them within
seconds. This needs full photo access; with a limited selection the app can
only see the photos you picked. Settings → Data → **Remove sample screenshots**
deletes them again.

### Does Toss Away delete my photos immediately?

No. Deleting a screenshot in Toss Away — from a group, in Quick review or with
Delete all — *stages* it; nothing is deleted yet. Everything waits in the app's
Trash until you review it and confirm. Deleting then goes through the standard
iOS dialog, and the photos stay in your **Recently Deleted** album for 30 days
afterwards.

### I deleted something by accident.

Open the Photos app → Albums → Recently Deleted. Anything removed in the last 30
days is recoverable there.

If it is still in Toss Away's Trash and you have not confirmed the delete yet,
just tap it to restore it.

### Why does the first scan take a while?

The app reads every screenshot on your device to sort it by content, and it does
that locally rather than on a server. On a large library the first pass takes a
few minutes; it runs in the background and pauses while Quick review is open.

On devices set to Japanese, Korean, Chinese or Russian it takes noticeably
longer — those scripts need a heavier text model.

### Do my photos get uploaded?

No. Nothing leaves your device. See the [privacy policy](privacy).

### What does Pro add?

Pro adds **Delete all** — clear a whole group in one tap — plus receipt export
to CSV and near-duplicate groups. Every smart category, search inside your
screenshots and one-by-one review are free, and there is no daily limit.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so they are cancelled in iPhone Settings →
your name → Subscriptions → Toss Away. Pro stays on until the paid week or year
runs out. The lifetime purchase has nothing to cancel.

### The Files tab does not show my downloads.

iOS only lets an app see a folder you hand it explicitly. Tap **Choose folder**
and pick one — for example Downloads in iCloud Drive. Toss Away cannot browse
your device's storage, and no iOS app can.

### Files I deleted in the Files tab are gone.

Yes — permanently. Documents do not have a Recently Deleted safety net the way
photos do, which is why that screen asks for confirmation and says so.
