## Common questions

### Does Toss Away delete my photos immediately?

No. Swiping left *stages* an item — it is not deleted. Everything waits in the
app's Trash until you review it and confirm. Deleting then goes through the
standard iOS dialog, and the photos stay in your **Recently Deleted** album for
30 days afterwards.

### I deleted something by accident.

Open the Photos app → Albums → Recently Deleted. Anything removed in the last 30
days is recoverable there.

If it is still in Toss Away's Trash and you have not confirmed the delete yet,
just tap it to restore it.

### Why does the first scan take a while?

The app reads every screenshot on your device to sort it by content, and it does
that locally rather than on a server. On a large library the first pass takes a
few minutes; it runs in the background and pauses while you are swiping.

On devices set to Japanese, Korean, Chinese or Russian it takes noticeably
longer — those scripts need a heavier text model.

### Do my photos get uploaded?

No. Nothing leaves your device. See the [privacy policy](privacy).

### What does Pro add?

Pro removes the 25-deletions-per-day limit and adds smart categories, grouping
of near-duplicates, and bulk delete. Grouping by time and by size is free and
always will be.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Toss Away. Cancelling there
stops renewal; you keep Pro until the current period ends.

### The Files tab does not show my downloads.

iOS only lets an app see a folder you hand it explicitly. Tap **Choose folder**
and pick one — for example Downloads in iCloud Drive. Toss Away cannot browse
your device's storage, and no iOS app can.

### Files I deleted in the Files tab are gone.

Yes — permanently. Documents do not have a Recently Deleted safety net the way
photos do, which is why that screen asks for confirmation and says so.
