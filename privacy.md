# Privacy Policy

**English** · [Español](es/privacy) · [Português](pt-br/privacy) · [Français](fr/privacy)

*Last updated: 27 August 2026*

Toss Away is a photo-library cleaner for iPhone. This policy explains exactly
what the app does and does not do with your data. It is short because the app
collects almost nothing.

## Your photos never leave your device

Toss Away reads your photo library to find screenshots, videos and photos, and
to measure how much space they take. It analyses them **entirely on your
iPhone**, using Apple's Vision framework.

No image, no recognised text, no filename and no thumbnail is ever transmitted,
uploaded or stored anywhere outside your device. There is no server, no account
and no sign-in.

## What is stored, and where

Everything Toss Away stores stays in the app's own container on your device:

- Which items you have kept or moved to trash
- Recognised text and an image fingerprint, used to group screenshots by content
  and to spot near-duplicates
- File sizes, so it can report how much space you would recover
- Your settings and a count of items cleared

Deleting the app removes all of it.

## Analytics

**Toss Away collects no analytics.** No usage events, no product metrics, no
crash reports, no advertising identifier. The app ships with no analytics SDK at
all, so there is nothing to opt out of.

It makes no network requests of its own. The only network traffic is Apple's own:
the App Store handling a purchase or a restore, and this page opening in your
browser if you tap the link inside the app.

## Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple. Toss Away never
sees your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether an entitlement is
active.

## Files you choose

If you use the Files feature, Toss Away can only see a folder you explicitly
pick through the iOS document picker. iOS gives apps no way to browse your
device's storage, and Toss Away does not attempt to. Files deleted there are
removed permanently — the app warns you before doing it.

## Deleting photos

Toss Away never deletes anything without your confirmation. Items you swipe away
are staged in the app's Trash. Deleting them goes through the standard iOS
confirmation dialog, after which the photos sit in your **Recently Deleted**
album for 30 days, exactly as they would if you deleted them in the Photos app.

## Children

Toss Away is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
